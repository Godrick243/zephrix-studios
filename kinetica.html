Kinetica

Digital Game - In Development

Kinetica is a 2D physics-based platformer built around using your momentum as a tool. It plays out in a big world with many vast biomes and buildings easy to get lost in. It's music is a mix of calm and rhythmic melancholic music made to immerse the player in the world. The bosses and enemies are beautifully designed with big optimization to make them as fun to face as possible while still keeping the core of the game in mind.

Core Idea

You dash around in a level slashing enemies and bouncing off walls while trying to escape these

Status

Early Prototype Phase
