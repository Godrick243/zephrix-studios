Battles of Minalria

Tabletop Game - In Development

Battles of Minalria is a competitive tebletop PvP game built around mana, positioning, and controlled escalation.

Players fight until only one team or player remains, using tactical card combat.

Core Idea

Last player or team standing wins, using all kinds of different types of combat: spells, traps, minions, swords, and so on.

Status

Prototype and early finialization phase
